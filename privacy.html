4. Visitor statistics (PostHog)

To understand how many people visit this website, where they come from (for example a link in an email signature or a post) and which links they use (for example which bookshop), I use PostHog, a web analytics service of PostHog Inc., USA. The statistics are processed on PostHog’s servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

PostHog runs in a cookieless mode: nothing is stored on or read from your device — no cookies, no local storage. To count a visit within one day, PostHog’s servers turn your IP address and browser identification, together with a random value, into a one-way code. The random value changes and is deleted every day, so visits cannot be linked across days or traced back to you, and your IP address is not stored in the statistics. Recorded are: the pages visited and how long they stay open, the referring website and campaign tags (such as utm_source), browser, operating system and screen size, and clicks on selected links (bookshops, downloads, email addresses and links to other websites). No form contents, no names or email addresses, no session recordings and no profiles. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signal, nothing is recorded.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — my legitimate interest in anonymous reach statistics to improve the website. As no information is stored on or read from your device, no consent is required under § 25 TDDDG. PostHog acts as my processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR); where PostHog staff outside the EU can access data, this is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The statistics are kept for at most 12 months. More: PostHog Privacy Policy.
